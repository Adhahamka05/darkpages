THE ATTIC WINDOW

The house at the end of the street has been empty since 1984. No power, no water, no neighbors. Yet, every night at 3:14 AM, a pale yellow light flickers in the attic window. I watched it from my bedroom with binoculars for a week until I saw it: a small, grey hand pressing against the glass.

The fingers were too long, and they moved with a rhythmic tapping that I could hear even through my closed windows. Yesterday, I finally entered the house. The air was thick with the smell of old copper. I climbed the stairs to the attic, but when I opened the door, there was no light. There was only a single chair facing the window. I felt a cold breath on my neck and heard a giggle. I turned around, but the door had vanished. I’m the one tapping on the glass now. Please, look up.
