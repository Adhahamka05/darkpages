THE MIRROR GUEST

The rule is simple: never look into a mirror in total darkness. I was a fool who didn't listen. I held a candle to the bathroom glass at midnight, waiting for a ghost. My reflection was there, normal at first. But when I blinked, my reflection didn't.

It stayed there, eyes wide, staring at me with a hunger I can't describe. Slowly, my reflection began to reach out—not toward the candle, but toward the glass itself. It tapped the mirror from the *inside*. I dropped the candle, and in the sudden pitch black, I felt a hand—not my own—grab my wrist. I managed to turn the lights on, but now my reflection is gone. The mirror is just a silver void. And I can feel someone watching me from every shiny surface in my house. I just saw a pair of eyes staring back at me from the screen of my phone.
