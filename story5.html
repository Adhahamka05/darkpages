THE FINAL BROADCAST

My grandfather’s old film projector started running by itself in the middle of the night. The light hit the wall, but instead of the old family movies, it showed a live feed of my own hallway. I watched the screen in horror as a tall, faceless figure walked toward my bedroom door.

I looked at my actual door—it was closed and silent. But on the wall, the figure reached out for the handle. The film started to melt, the edges of the image turning into a bubbling black mess. Just before the projector died, the figure turned its head toward the camera and whispered my phone number. A second later, my phone lit up with a call from 'Unknown.' I haven't answered it yet, but the projector has started up again, and this time, the figure is sitting at the foot of my bed on the screen.
